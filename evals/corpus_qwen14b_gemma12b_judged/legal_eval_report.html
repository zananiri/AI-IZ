Issues and recommended fixes

Ordered by how many points each is costing. File references are in this repository.

CriticalLegislation from outside Israel proper is polluting retrieval

Evidence: Judea & Samaria military orders were retrieved for 18 of 100 questions, the Jordanian Criminal Law for 9–11, the repealed 1984 Civil Procedure Regulations for 9–10, and the Herut movement's 1948 draft constitution for 4–8. They produced wrong answers on IL-306 (age of responsibility cited to Jordanian law), IL-321 (detention sent to the "military court of appeals"), IL-371, and IL-388. In case 02, the Protected Housing Law (retirement homes) was applied to an ordinary lease.

Fix: tag each record with jurisdiction and status when the corpus is built (scripts/legal_data/). Exclude military orders, foreign law, drafts and proposals (הצעת…), and repealed instruments, or filter them at query time in legal/corpus_retrieval.py.

CriticalExact legal terms miss their section

Evidence: the gold section was retrieved for only 79–84% of questions, and each miss roughly halves the score. Examples of misses: "הדבר מעיד על עצמו" (Torts Ordinance §41), "דרישת הסבירות" (Penal Law §34טז, which was confused with reasonable doubt, §34כב), "עושק" (Contracts Law §18), Limitation Law §9, and Land Law §9. For the Freedom of Information question the FOI Law was never retrieved at all.

Fix: add hybrid lexical search. The lexical_*.jsonl files are already built by the vectorizer but go unused at query time. Add a term-to-section table for statutory terms of art, and boost sections whose heading matches a quoted term.

SeriousThe retrieval planner truncates and plans the wrong issue

Evidence: the planner's JSON was cut off at 512 tokens once for qwen and four times for gemma, and each time the question fell back to an unplanned search. Issues are written in English for Hebrew questions. For IL-001 (contract formation) the plan searched for contract termination.

Fix: raise PLAN_MAX_TOKENS (legal/corpus_retrieval.py:49) to 1024, ask for issues and law names in Hebrew, and cap the length of each issue.

Seriousgemma opens almost every answer with כן or לא

Evidence: 55 of 66 non-yes/no answers begin with a stray "כן," or "לא,". On yes/no items the reflex scored IL-016, IL-214 and IL-219 as wrong: the reasoning is correct but the first word contradicts it. That costs 1.5 points outright and makes answers read badly.

Fix: tell the eval prompt to open with כן/לא only when the question itself is a yes/no question (scripts/legal_data/eval_run.py, RAG_SYSTEM), or strip the opener after generation for other categories.

Seriousqwen's script leaks survive the Hebrew rewrite

Evidence: 26 of qwen's 100 final answers still contain Cyrillic, Arabic, Chinese, Thai or stray Latin fragments, even after 24 hebrew_rewrite passes. Examples: "имеет", "أشهر", "杀人", "הhudעה". In one case file the seller's name came out as "ספות ב клиיק". gemma had none.

Fix: re-run the deterministic script check (legal/script_check.py) after the rewrite and repeat until clean, or let a non-thinking model produce the final wording.

SeriousCase files invent deadlines and facts

Evidence: deadlines averaged 35–36% of their points. The models invented limitation periods: "one year and a day", "90 days to file", "dismissal + 60 days" (printed three times), and a "limitation date" of 15.9.2026. qwen named the wrong beneficiary in case 05 and reversed the order of the two sales in case 04. gemma left out the requested document in case 05.

Fix: the new Legal-tab case mode (run_case_turn) already requires a cited provision for every period and verifies each citation. Still to add: a deterministic date calculator, a facts-consistency check against the case file, and a completeness check that all eight sections are present.

WarningAmendment questions fail without version history

Evidence: the amendments category scored 38–50%. Both models denied the 2024–26 amendments to Arrests Law §17 (IL-347), and qwen dated the durable power of attorney to 1962 (IL-198).

Fix: store version dates and amendment links in the corpus metadata. The bulk corpus has no amendment index, so _amendment_notes returns nothing on this path.

WarningMost of the run time is CPU retrieval

Evidence: gemma's run took 168 minutes wall-clock, but only 44 minutes of that was model calls. About 74% was embedding and reranking on the CPU, because the notebook hides the GPU from retrieval (CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=""). For qwen it was about 42%.

Fix: run bge-m3 and the reranker on the T4. Both fit beside a 12B Q4 model in 15 GB. That would cut gemma's eval time roughly in half.

NextEvaluate the production pipeline, not just the baseline

Evidence: this run used the eval harness's single-call path (legal_eval_baseline), not run_legal_turn with its memorandum, draft and entailment checks. Misgrounded citations (20 answers for qwen, 13 for gemma) are exactly what that verifier removes.

Fix: add a run mode that answers through run_legal_turn / run_case_turn, then compare it against this baseline.

How this was scored

  • Questions are the eval's fixed stratified sample: 100 of the 500 in questions.jsonl, seed 2026, across 9 categories. The 16 multiple-choice items and the 18 yes/no labels are scored automatically by the eval's own score.py.
  • The other 84 answers per model were graded by Claude using the eval's Prompt A. Correctness is scored 0/1/2 against the gold answer, key points and citations, with grounding and hallucination flags. Both models' answers were graded side by side for consistency, and score.py report computed the totals. Case files were graded with Prompt B against cases_gold.json, penalties included. qwen's case 04 scored −2 after penalties and was floored at 0.
  • This was a single grading pass, and the eval's guide recommends spot-checking 30–50 items with a lawyer. IL-247 is flagged for review: its gold answer is medium-confidence and the models disagree on the section.
  • Retrieval hits were checked by matching each gold citation (law and base section) against the model's retrieved chunks.
