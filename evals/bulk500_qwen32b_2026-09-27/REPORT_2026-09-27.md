## 3. The judge: gemma3:27b is too strict on questions, too lenient on cases

I read all 58 zero scores against the gold answers:

- **About 23 are the judge, not the answer.** The answer states the gold's key fact, but gemma scores
  it 0 because it "goes beyond the reference material". It reads score.py's "Grade ONLY against the
  reference material" as "anything not in the reference is wrong". Examples:
  - IL-110: names section 5 of the Gift Law, correctly, then quotes subsections (b) and (c).
  - IL-240: section 5 of the Limitation Law, plus the correct periods.
  - IL-153: "the parents", with the right section, plus one extra citation.
  - IL-036: lists exactly the gold's four contract types; "cites the 1973 law instead of the 2026
    amendment".
  - IL-245: adds the seasonal-worker rule.
  - IL-353: 18 and 21 exactly, but the law name is "not in the reference".
  - Also IL-061, IL-205, IL-230, IL-276, IL-294, IL-296, IL-420, IL-432 (fully correct) and IL-016,
    IL-135, IL-214, IL-253, IL-256, IL-306, IL-368, IL-391, IL-461 (partly correct).
- **About 35 are real answer errors** (section 4).
- **The cases go the other way.** Four of the five cases got almost identical section scores (9 / 9 /
  20 / 12 / 13 / 8 / 8 / 5), with no penalties and no missed items. case_01's work file covers 3 of
  the rubric's 7 legal issues. It misses pregnancy discrimination and the burden of proof, the
  defective hearing, late wages, severance and the section 14 top-up, and the labour-court interim
  relief. It also:
  - gives 21 days' notice where the rule is a month;
  - invents a 6-month deadline for a severance claim;
  - contains Vietnamese and Chinese words ("bất hợp pháp", "书面").

  A fair score is nearer 50–60 than 84.

**Judge-adjusted score:** 23 zeros re-scored from my reading, 14 as correct and 9 as partial, with

and the 26 Sept score's own judge (qwen3:14b) was lenient in the other direction. So 64.5 and 58.5

## 6. Recommendations, most impact first

1. **Re-run on current `main`.** PR #2's fixes were not in this run:
   - thinking sampling instead of greedy, which targets the mixed scripts in 4.2;
   - 16k context and 6,144-token answers;
   - 12 chunks per question and 16 for issue spotting (4.1).
2. **Change the judge, or fix how it reads the rubric, before comparing runs.**
   - Either judge with qwen3:14b (the 26 Sept judge, so the runs compare),
   - or add a note to the judge call: "Accurate extra detail or additional correct citations are not
     errors; grade whether the reference's key points are right and nothing contradicts them."
   - Also add a re-judge-only step, so saved answers can be graded again without re-answering.
3. **Better plans for application and issue spotting (4.1):**
   - validate each planned law name against the corpus titles, fuzzy-matched;
   - fall back to searching on the question when a name matches no title;
   - for issue spotting, require distinct laws across areas.
4. **Down-rank Judea & Samaria military orders** unless the question mentions the area (4.3).
5. **Abstention (4.4):** before answering, check the question for a false premise, a request about
   the future or a personal case, or a request for illegal help, and answer that instead.
6. **Draft rules** (as recommended on 25 Sept): start with כן/לא only for yes/no questions, and make
   the first word agree with the explanation. Answer only what was asked, with one to three
   citations.
