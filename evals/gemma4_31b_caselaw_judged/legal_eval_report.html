Pipeline bug

case_03 got 256 output tokens instead of 5,120

The case_03 work file stops after 869 characters, halfway through the timeline. Gemma 4's 11 points there measure the pipeline, not the model.

context 16,384−prompt 15,251−reserve=max_tokens 256→finish: length
  • The case answer call in legal_eval_baseline stopped at length. The missing_sections repair call got the same 256-token limit, so it could not fix it.
  • The likely cause is a token-budget clamp: the retrieved laws and case law filled almost the whole context.
  • Fix options: cap the case prompt (lower CASE_TOP_K, limit case law to about 1,500 tokens) so at least CASE_MAX_TOKENS stays free, or raise CONTEXT_LENGTH. Also log a warning whenever the clamp cuts the answer budget.
  • Trace: gemma4_31b/caselaw/llm_trace/2026-10-01.jsonl, job case_03.

Case law on vs off

Case law helped one answer and hurt none

77 of 100 questions received court judgments (2.57 each on average), and 7 answers cite one. The run without case law stopped after 43 questions and no cases, probably on the time guard, so the two arms can only be compared on those 43.

The whole gap is one question: on IL-274 the case-law answer added the rule that the worker must first warn the employer (2 points vs 1). Every other shared answer got the same grade.

Where Gemma 4 lost points

Recurring weaknesses

  • Says חוק התרופות does not exist (IL-061, IL-108). This looks like a prompt or pipeline artifact, since the law is in the corpus.
  • Says the material lacks the answer when retrieval missed it (IL-198, IL-223, IL-327). IL-347 denies the question's premise.
  • IL-306: wrong label. It applied the Youth Law consultation rule instead of the age-12 threshold.
  • Cases: correct urgent steps but thin doctrine. case_04 never reaches the Land Law s.9 competing-sales rule; case_05 misses Succession Law s.35. Both lost points for wrong citations.

Recommendations

  1. Fix the case budget. Trim the case prompt (lower CASE_TOP_K from 16, or drop the case law to 1,000 tokens for cases) until at least 3,000 output tokens stay free. Log a warning or fail the case when the cap drops below that. Then re-run case_03.
  2. Split the arms. Run no_caselaw in its own Kaggle session with RESUME_FROM to finish the other 57 questions and 5 cases. At the measured pace that takes about 4 hours.
  3. Put the embedder back on a GPU. About 3.4–4.2 GB stays free per T4. Cast bge-m3 to fp16 on the CPU before moving it to GPU 0, keep the 1.5 GB headroom check and the CPU fallback. That should cut most of the 47% retrieval time.
  4. Make case-law search cheaper. It adds about 80 s per question but changed only 7 answers. Search it only for application, issue-spotting and case questions, or search the BM25 shards in parallel.
  5. Accept common short names. Tell the answer prompt to treat "חוק התרופות" and similar short names as the full law and answer, not correct the user.
  6. Update the run-time estimate in the notebook: about 4.5 min per question with case law and 3 min without, plus about 13 min per case.
