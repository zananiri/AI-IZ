- **Quote cut-offs are gone** (B5).

  אחד…".

  1,800 characters per question, none of the passes ran out of room, and the notes are readable.

## 4. What went wrong, and why

The same answer-writing changes that fixed q13 and q18 cost points elsewhere, for five reasons.

| # | Cause | Evidence | Questions hit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | **The citation verifier became too strict.** The new instruction to "judge the sentence as written", combined with the explanation-first order, makes it reject correct sentences. | not-entailed rate **11% → 26%**; sentences removed **4 → 17**; redrafts **4 → 20** | q05, B7, B2, q11, q08 |
| 4.2 | **The "answer first: yes or no, the number, …" rule is misread** as "always start with yes or no" | "לא," as the answer to *which dates*; "כן," to *how long* | q05, q06, q24 |
| 4.3 | **Longer, padded answers:** restating the question, naming laws and sections in the prose, numbered recaps, speculative "the law doesn't say…" sentences | median answer length **126 → 207** characters; the judge flags them as "fabricated specifics" | q06, q07, q22, q23, q24, q11, q08 |
| 4.4 | **More mixed-script words**, likely leaking from the English reasoning notes into the memo and draft (הобязанность, октяבר, 서비스, WAY, testify, Público, נchezות) | "Words in another script remain" escalations **2 → 11** | q05, q06, q08, q10, q22, B2, C2 |
| 4.5 | **A "not stated" finding has no path to an answer.** The empty memo fails the gate, and the user gets the gate-failure notice. | the reasoning notes said NOT STATED | q19 |
| 4.6 | **The draft invents a source ID** (`S1`) when forced to add citations | a structural failure, so the answer was withheld | q10 |
| 4.7 | **A draft cut short after a lead-in** (probably an ASCII quote in a law name) | the draft ends in ":" and has no citations | q16 |
| 4.8 | **Missing domain knowledge:** which Arrests Law section governs detention "until the end of proceedings" | the reasoning notes chose 26(3)–(5) | q04 |

**Escalations:** 28 of the 40 turns were escalated for attorney review (10 of 38 on 24 Sept). Most are
side effects of 4.1 and 4.4, plus memos that now fill `authority_conflicts` with non-conflicts
("אין סתירה…").

**Time:** 56 s → **109–118 s per question**, so the Kaggle run took about 75 minutes instead of about
38. Of the new time, the reasoning pass accounts for about 36 s per question and the extra
verification calls for most of the rest (147 checks instead of 87, because of the extra sentences and
redrafts).

## 5. The judge

Explanation-first grading is still inconsistent. The 14B judge keeps calling a correctly named law or
section a "fabricated specific" (q06, q07, q22, q24), even though its rubric says cited laws never
count as specifics. The narrower contradiction check found nothing contradicting the gold answer in
these cases, so each was capped at "partial" rather than "incorrect". Without the re-grade it's
unknown how many of the "correct → partial" moves are the judge rather than the answers. For q06, q07,
q22 and q24 the answers did get worse too (see 4.2 and 4.3), so both factors are likely at work.

## 6. Recommendations, most impact first

1. **Relax the verifier and guard removals.** Drop "judge the sentence as written…" from the
   verification prompt (the fragment check already catches lead-ins deterministically). Don't remove a
   sentence whose words and numbers nearly all appear in the cited source: keep it and mark it
   unverified instead. Expected to recover q05, B7 and B2, cut redrafts, and bring back about 30 s per
   question.
2. **Tighten the draft instructions:**
   - start with yes/no only when the question is a yes/no question;
   - never restate the question;
   - no law names or section numbers in the prose (the citations carry them);
   - say what the law doesn't state only when the question asks about that;
   - no numbered recaps.

   Targets q05, q06, q07, q22, q23, q24 and the "fabricated" flags.
3. **Give "not stated" its own answer.** When the memo has no claims and the reasoning says NOT
   STATED, reply "the indexed law does not state this" in the question's language, instead of the
   gate-failure notice. Targets q19 and similar questions.
4. **Write the reasoning notes in the question's language** (the thinking itself can stay English), to
   cut mixed-script leakage.
5. **Repair invented source IDs** using the memo's own claim → source pairs (q10). **Detect drafts cut
   off at a lead-in** and retry them with the non-greedy sampling (q16).
6. **Tell the judge explicitly** that naming the law or section a rule comes from is never a
   fabricated specific. Then run the re-grade, using the repo's notebook, to measure the judge
   separately.
7. **Re-import `notebooks/kaggle_legal_eval.ipynb` from the repo into Kaggle**, to get the 16k
   context, the trace files and `*_trace.md`, and the re-grade step.
